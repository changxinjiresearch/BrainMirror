Further operator-specific magnitude and imaging-artifact controls are required before scientific evidence-dependence claims.

## 04A02 screening operations clarification — source mask containment

During the first all-VAL source scan, an original P1 WT mask was found to extend outside the separately stored P1 brain-foreground mask (`Original WT is not contained within original brain foreground`). This is a **per-patient failure of the original 04A-v1 strict ROI eligibility precondition**, not automatically proof that the NPZ or glioma segmentations are corrupted.

The screen now records the **unchanged original WT pixels**, foreground pixel count, the absolute count/fraction of WT pixels outside the foreground, and a distinct `INELIGIBLE_ORIGINAL_V1_MASK_CONTAINMENT` reason. The patient is never counted `ELIGIBLE`; no ROI is chosen, no original WT/foreground mask is intersected, eroded, filled, regenerated or rescaled; the original `choose()` scientific and numerical requirements, including the cutoff `2.0`, remain unchanged. Where the original mask is empty or the foreground too small, it is independently counted as `INELIGIBLE_ORIGINAL_V1_EMPTY_WT_OR_TOO_SMALL_FOREGROUND`. Nonbinary/invalid shapes, NaNs, unexpected source errors and bad SHA256 remain fatal to the whole scan rather than being silently relabeled.

This clarification only changes **how the completeness audit handles and documents ineligible cases** after detecting them. It does not grant permission to use any original-v1-ineligible subject for a new model experiment. Report all 55 screened, eligible and ineligible, with distinct mask geometry attrition counts and all original source checksums in private GitHub.
